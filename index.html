Hi! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Song-Chun Zhu. I am also a research intern at Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI), supervised by Dr. Yixin Chen and Dr. Siyuan Huang. Before beginning my Ph.D. study, I received my B.S. in the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beihang University. My research interests lie in computer vision, especially 3D/4D scene modeling, video generation, and world models. I warmly welcome academic discussions and collaborations!
